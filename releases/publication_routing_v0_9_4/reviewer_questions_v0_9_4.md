# Reviewer Questions v0.9.4

## Q1. Is the contribution framed as evidence governance rather than semiconductor validation?

Why it matters: This is the central boundary condition.

## Q2. Are the source categories and validation gaps legible to a reader outside the project?

Why it matters: Publication value depends on external readability.

## Q3. Does the evidence table make it impossible to confuse source-populated with source-validated?

Why it matters: This prevents accidental overclaiming.

## Q4. Are the non-claim locks too conservative, too weak, or correctly calibrated?

Why it matters: Over-restraint can obscure the contribution; under-restraint creates false validation.

## Q5. Which route is most appropriate: GitHub release, technical note, Gist, or preprint?

Why it matters: Publication channel should match evidence maturity.
